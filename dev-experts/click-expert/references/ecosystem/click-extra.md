# Click Extra

Cloup plus a colorful `--help`, and ready-made `--config`, `--show-params` and
`--verbosity` options. Reach for it before hand-wiring a config file into `default_map`
(`../parameters/value-resolution.md`).

Docs: https://github.com/kdeldycke/click-extra
